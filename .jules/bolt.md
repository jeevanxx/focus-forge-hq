## 2025-08-02 - Single-Pass Date Mapping for Period Aggregations
**Learning:** Calling timezone-safe parsing/formatting (`parseISO` and `format` from `date-fns`) inside nested filtering structures (like nested `map` over weekdays or intervals, resulting in O(M*N) complexity) is extremely CPU-heavy because it parses each session's date multiple times. Initializing a predefined mapping of dates to array indices in O(M) time and resolving session dates in a single-pass O(N) loop yields a massive ~7-8x speedup (~572ms down to ~72ms for 10,000 sessions).
**Action:** When aggregating records over a fixed interval (such as a 7-day week), pre-populate the target intervals first and construct an index lookup map. Then, process the incoming dataset exactly once (O(N)), avoiding nested filters and redundant parsing.
