## 2026-10-22 - Single-Pass Map Bucketing for Timezone-Aware Weekly Focus Analytics
**Learning:** `getWeeklyFocus` originally iterated through the list of focus sessions 7 times (once for each day of the week), parsing ISO dates and formatting them on every iteration (O(7N)). Simply doing string slicing on ISO dates (e.g. `s.date.slice(0, 10)`) introduces subtle timezone bugs for dates non-aligned with UTC. Retaining `format(parseISO(s.date), 'yyyy-MM-dd')` while grouping into a single-pass `Map<string, number>` iteration reduces complexity to O(N) while maintaining local timezone accuracy.
**Action:** Always prefer grouping into a single-pass `Map` or hash table for date-bucketed aggregations while preserving timezone formatting methods like `format(parseISO(...))` to guarantee correctness.
